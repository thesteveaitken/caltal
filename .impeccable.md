## Design Context

### Users
- Steve and a few friends. Adults who already track food and want it to take seconds, not minutes.
- Used on a phone, standing up, right after eating or drinking. Often one-handed, often several times a day. Occasionally on a laptop to look back at Trends.
- Job to be done: record what I just ate or drank with as few taps as possible, then glance at where the day stands against my limit and goals. Over weeks: see whether I've been keeping to it.

### Brand Personality
- Three words: **quick, easy, accountable**.
- Accountable means the app actively helps you hold yourself to your own goals: it keeps the goal in view, makes where you stand unmissable, and makes the record easy to look back on. It does that as a fair witness, not a coach.
- Voice: a scorekeeper or a good bookkeeper. Plain, dry, matter-of-fact. States the number and lets it speak. Never cheerleads (no confetti, no "Great job!"), never scolds or shames. Over the limit is a fact on the page, not a moral failing.
- Emotional goal: the small satisfaction of making a mark and seeing the tally add up; the steadiness of a well-kept book.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Territory: **Ledger**. A pocket notebook, a bar tab, a bookkeeper's day book. Entries are line items on ruled rows with a running total, not stacks of cards. Figures are the hero and are set like figures in a ledger: aligned, tabular, confident.
- The logo stays as it is (three upright strokes struck through; Steve likes it). It is the brand's one graphic idea. Use the tally mark where counting actually happens (logged items, progress, empty states), not as decoration.
- Colour: one brand hue in an ink-highlighter / pencil-mark role. The current amber is open to change; if it changes, the logo's stroke colour follows it but the mark's form does not. Neutrals tinted toward the brand hue. Nutrient colours cut back to as few as possible; colour only where it carries meaning (over the limit, goal met).
- Theme: both light and dark, following the device setting. Light is "paper" (the primary expression of the ledger); dark is the same book in ink, not a separate design.
- Anti-references: ad-cluttered mainstream calorie apps; gamified streak/confetti fitness apps; pastel wellness apps; generic dark dashboards (bordered rounded card around everything, rainbow metric colours, system font).
- Constraints: one static `index.html`, vanilla CSS/JS, no build step. Fonts from Google Fonts (or self-hosted). Mobile-first; desktop widens to two columns at 900px.

### Accessibility
- WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 for text (including small/secondary text), 3:1 for UI and chart marks.
- Pinch-zoom allowed. Visible keyboard focus. Icon-only buttons have labels.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Never encode meaning in colour alone (over/under also shown by text or shape).

### Design Principles
1. **The log is the product.** Adding an entry is always one reach away, at any point in the day. Nothing outranks it on screen.
2. **Rows, not cards.** Lists are ruled line items. Frames are reserved for the one or two things that really are objects (the day's total, a sheet).
3. **Numbers first.** Figures get the best typography on the page; labels are quiet. One type voice for figures, one for words.
4. **Colour means something.** Amber marks the brand and the live state; red-ish only for over. Everything else is ink and paper.
5. **Keep score, don't keep tabs on feelings.** Copy is short and factual. Show where you stand; never praise or scold.

### Decisions so far (11 Nov 2026, canvas round 2)
- Base: direction B ("Bar tab"). Paper backgrounds: light `#f5f5f2`, dark `#15171b`. Fonts: Barlow Condensed (figures, wordmark) + Barlow (text).
- Brand hue stays amber. Dark mode `#e8a23d`. Light mode `#b87415` for marks/bars/logo strokes, `#9a5d08` for amber text.
- Over the limit: light `#9b1b4a`, dark `#f0708a`, plus a thin goal marker after the 20th mark, shown only once over (never with no calorie goal).
- Hero: calories so far, large ("785 / 2,200"); calories left is secondary on the right ("1,415 left", or "352 over" in the over colour).
- Tally: 20 marks = the calorie goal (struck in fives, four strokes close together, strike overhanging both sides). The 20 marks take 2/3 of the width so up to 150% fits on one line; beyond that it wraps. The mark in progress fills from the bottom. Marks past the goal use the over colour.
- No calorie goal: tally as if the goal were 2,000, marks keep going past it, always amber, no marker, no "left".
- Goal grid, 3 across (5 on desktop): goals that are on (protein, fluids, creatine) with a progress bar, then carbs and fat as plain figures without bars. Hidden goals drop out and the rest slide up.
- Search + quick-add: direction C (outlined search field, "Quick:" amber text links), pinned above the tab bar.

### Decisions (round 3, sheets and Trends)
- Sheets: paper bottom sheet with a handle; ruled rows; steppers share one fixed width so − and + line up across rows. Primary button is amber `#e8a23d` fill with ink text in both themes.
- Log sheet shows one line under the calories: "After this: 1,135 cal left" or "After this: 352 cal over".
- Editing an entry: "Save changes" plus "Remove entry" (delete leaves the row); removal shows an Undo toast.
- New food: primary "Save and log 1 serving", secondary "Save only". Same form for one-offs.
- Profile menu shows current values (Goals "2,200 cal", Quick-add "3 of 6").
- Trends, 7 days: ruled sections, avg + days-at-goal figures, bars aligned to the same day columns. Calories amber, over-limit days crimson; protein/fluids grey, amber on days at goal; carbs dark grey `#555b63` (dark `#b9bec6`) + fat light grey; creatine amber dots; alcohol by day. Today is a dashed outline; averages leave out today. Weekly alcohol only on the 4 weeks view.
- Guest mode: one-line strip ("Saved on this device only · Sign in"), not the large banner.
- Theme follows the device; no manual toggle for now. Food-list ordering (recents first) is a later pass.
