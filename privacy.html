Privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

CalTal is a small, non-commercial calorie and macro tracker, run as a personal project and shared with friends. This page explains what it stores, where, and how to have it removed. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Using CalTal without signing in

If you don't sign in, everything you log stays in your browser's local storage on that device. It is not sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data deletes it. The standard food list is downloaded from the database, but nothing about you is sent with that request.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in with Google, CalTal receives your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account. It uses them only to sign you in, keep your data separate from other people's, and show who is signed in. CalTal asks for nothing else from Google: it cannot see your contacts, files, mail or anything else in your Google account.

If you used CalTal on the same device before signing in, what you logged there is copied into your account when you sign in.

What is stored for signed-in users

  • Your food log: what you ate or drank, how much, and when.
  • Foods you add, your favourites and your quick-add buttons.
  • Your goals (calories, protein, fluids, creatine, goal weight).
  • Weigh-ins you enter.
  • If you use the goal helper: birth year, sex, height, weight, activity level and aim, which it uses to suggest goals.

Some of this, such as weight, is health-related. It is used only to show you your own log, totals and trends.

Where it is kept and who can see it

Signed-in data is stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Database rules make each person's rows readable and writable only by that person; other users cannot see them. As the person who runs CalTal, I can access the database to keep it running, and I don't look at individual people's data except to fix a problem or act on a request from you.

Other services handle your requests in the normal way when the app loads. They may see your IP address and browser details, under their own policies:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.
  • jsDelivr serves the Supabase code library.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • Google handles sign-in.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it or ask for it to be deleted. You can remove individual entries, foods and weigh-ins in the app at any time. To delete your whole account and everything in it, or to get a copy of your data, email caltalapp@gmail.com from the address you sign in with. I'll do it within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also remove CalTal's access to your Google account at any time from your Google account connections.

Use of Google user data

CalTal's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

CalTal is not meant for children under 13, and its goal helper does not suggest goals for anyone under 18.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Questions go to caltalapp@gmail.com. See also the terms of service.
